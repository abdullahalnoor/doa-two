# Nanu's Bhapa Pitha Recipe

## Ingredients
- 2 cups rice flour
- 1 cup grated coconut
- - 1.5 cups date palm jaggery (gur)
- A pinch of salt
- Water as needed

## Instructions
1. Mix the rice flour with a pinch of salt.
2. Sprinkle water gradually until the flour becomes crumbly.
3. Place some rice flour in a pitha mold.
4. Add grated coconut and date palm jaggery.
5. Cover with more rice flour.
6. Steam until cooked.
7. Serve warm with family.

**Special note from Nanu:** Make every pitha with love.
